**pVR** is an alignment-free genomic classification framework that combines p-adic hierarchical structure and k-mer composition within a bi-filtered Vietoris–Rips complex. It provides stability and prime-invariance guarantees, extracts topological features for machine learning, and achieves strong performance on several low-sample genomic benchmarks, outperforming established baselines and frozen foundation-model embeddings.
